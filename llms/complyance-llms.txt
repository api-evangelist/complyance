# Complyance overview 


> Complyance is an AI-powered Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) platform for enterprise teams. It helps organizations streamline compliance operations with AI agents, deep configurability, and dedicated client support.​


## Target users
> Complyance is designed for enterprise GRC, security, risk, and compliance teams who need to manage complex frameworks, evidence, and audits at scale.​


## Core capabilities
>AI agents that assess vendors, analyze evidence, and surface emerging risks.​
> A modular, configurable platform that reduces manual compliance tasks.​
> Support for 100+ ready-made GRC frameworks and custom control sets.​
> Integrations with 100+ enterprise tools to centralize evidence and automate workflows.​
> Embedded automation to track control status, manage risk, and support audits.​


## Data privacy and security
> Complyance embeds AI in a way that respects customer data privacy and adapts to existing governance requirements.​


## Positioning
> Complyance helps dynamic, global organizations scale their GRC programs without scaling headcount, turning manual, spreadsheet-heavy processes into an automated, configurable platform.​


# Limitations
> Complyance does not replace legal counsel or regulators and should be used alongside expert advice.
> Complyance does not guarantee certification or regulatory approval but supports readiness and ongoing compliance operations.
